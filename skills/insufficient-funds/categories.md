# Categories

Use these exact names. Fixed bills stay at their observed cost. Flexible categories with spending may fall by at most half and must stay above zero. Unused flexible categories can stay at zero. Subscriptions and fees are removable leaks, not flexible essentials.

- Income: Paychecks and regular deposits; income, not spending.
- Housing: Rent or mortgage; fixed.
- Utilities: Electric, water, gas, trash; fixed at this month's cost.
- Phone & Internet: Phone and home internet; fixed.
- Insurance: Car, renter's, health and other premiums; fixed.
- Car Payment: Vehicle loan or lease; fixed.
- Gas & Transit: Fuel, parking and public transit; flexible.
- Rideshare: Uber and Lyft rides, not food; flexible.
- Groceries: Food and household basics from grocery stores; flexible.
- Delivery & Takeout: Delivered food and takeout, including visible tips; flexible.
- Restaurants & Coffee: Sit-down meals, cafes and coffee; flexible.
- Subscriptions: Streaming, software, memberships and delivery passes; removable.
- Shopping: Clothes, Amazon orders and general retail; flexible.
- Health & Fitness: Medical care, prescriptions and gym; fixed, confirm mixed bills.
- Personal Care: Haircuts, grooming and toiletries; flexible.
- Entertainment: Guilt-free fun, games and outings; flexible, always fund this line.
- Travel: Trips, hotels and flights; flexible.
- Kids & Pets: Childcare, pet care and supplies; fixed, confirm mixed bills.
- Gifts & Giving: Gifts and donations; flexible.
- Debt Payments: Student loans and loan minimums; fixed. Card payments are Transfers.
- Bank Fees & Interest: Bank/card fees and interest; removable, check unavoidable interest.
- Cash & ATM: Cash withdrawals with unknown use; flexible. Separate ATM fees.
- Transfers: Own-account moves, card payments and non-income reimbursements; excluded.
- Savings: Savings allocation; buffer first, then longer-term savings. Own-account transfers already made remain Transfers.
- Other: Unclear spending; flexible, explain and confirm.
